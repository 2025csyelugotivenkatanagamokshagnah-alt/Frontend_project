Workshop Introduction

An engineering workshop is a place where students learn practical engineering skills. It contains different machines and tools used for cutting, drilling, shaping and joining materials.

Workshop practice helps students understand manufacturing processes and develop practical knowledge about engineering machines.

Engineering Machines

Lathe Machine

A lathe machine rotates a workpiece while a cutting tool removes unwanted material from it.

It is used for turning, facing, drilling, threading and cutting cylindrical objects.

It is commonly used to make shafts, bolts, screws, rods and other machine components.

Drilling Machine

A drilling machine is used to make circular holes in materials such as metal, wood and plastic.

A rotating drill bit moves into the workpiece and produces a hole of the required size.

It is useful for making holes for screws, bolts and machine parts.

Welding Machine

A welding machine is used to join two or more metal parts permanently using heat.

The heat melts the joining area and creates a strong connection when the metal cools.

Welding is used for frames, gates, pipes, structures and machine components.

Workshop Safety Rules

  • Always wear safety goggles.
  • Wear proper protective clothing.
  • Use machines only with instructions.
  • Keep the workshop clean.
  • Do not touch moving machine parts.
  • Keep hands away from cutting tools.
  • Switch off machines after use.
  • Follow the instructor's instructions.
