Workshop Introduction

An engineering workshop is a place where students learn practical engineering skills. It provides tools and machines for cutting, drilling, welding and other activities.

Engineering Tools

Lathe Machine

Used for cutting and shaping rotating materials.

Drilling Machine

Used for making holes in different materials.

Welding Machine

Used for joining metal parts together.

Workshop Safety Rules

  • Wear safety goggles while working.
  • Use tools carefully and correctly.
  • Keep the workshop clean.
  • Wear proper protective clothing.
  • Switch off machines after use.
