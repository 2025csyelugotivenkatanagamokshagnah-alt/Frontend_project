Healthy Lifestyle

Maintain a daily routine, stay active and avoid unhealthy habits. A positive lifestyle keeps our body and mind healthy.

Healthy Food

Eat fresh fruits, vegetables, whole grains and healthy proteins. Avoid eating too much junk food.

Exercise

Regular exercise keeps the body strong and improves our energy and mood.

Sleep & Water Tips

  • Get 7–9 hours of sleep.
  • Drink enough water every day.
  • Take breaks while studying.
  • Follow a regular sleep schedule.

Health Awareness

Regular checkups and healthy habits help us take better care of our health. Never ignore unusual health problems.
