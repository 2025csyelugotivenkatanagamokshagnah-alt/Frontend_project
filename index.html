Workshop Introduction

An engineering workshop is a place where students learn practical engineering skills. It provides tools and machines for cutting, drilling, welding and other activities.

Workshop practice helps students understand how machines work and how engineering materials can be shaped and joined.

Engineering Machines

Lathe Machine

A lathe machine is used to rotate a workpiece and remove unwanted material using a cutting tool.

Uses: It is used for turning, cutting, threading and shaping cylindrical objects.

Examples: Shafts, rods, screws and metal components.

Drilling Machine

A drilling machine is used to make round holes in materials such as metal, wood and plastic.

Uses: It is mainly used for drilling holes and enlarging existing holes.

Examples: Machine parts, metal plates and wooden parts.

Welding Machine

A welding machine is used to join two or more metal parts permanently using heat or electrical energy.

Uses: It is used for joining metal sheets, pipes and different metal components.

Examples: Frames, gates, pipes and machine structures.

Workshop Safety Rules

  • Wear safety goggles while working.
  • Use tools carefully and correctly.
  • Keep the workshop clean.
  • Wear proper protective clothing.
  • Switch off machines after use.
  • Follow the instructions given by the instructor.
  • Do not touch moving machine parts.
